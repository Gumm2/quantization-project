TinyLlama's first query-projection layer has most of its weights packed very close to zero, but there are still some extreme outliers. About 0.061% of the weights are more than 10 standard deviations from zero, and the largest weight is about 96.7 standard deviations away. Even though these weights make up a tiny part of the layer, they can have a huge effect on global symmetric quantization because the largest absolute weight determines the scale.
